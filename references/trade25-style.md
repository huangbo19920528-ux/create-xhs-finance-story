# Personal Finance Experience / Trade25 Style

Use this mode for a first-person review of a bank account or feature, fee plan, app function, cross-border payment experience, securities-market access, promotion, eligibility rule, or other financial tool. It is modeled on the successful post `汇丰Trade25：35岁前值得考虑的选择之一`, but the reusable method is the judgment process, not its exact outline.

Reference specimen: `https://www.xiaohongshu.com/explore/6a866d94000000002100f53b`

## Positioning

Write as an ordinary user who actually used or seriously researched the service and made a considered judgment from personal experience plus public information.

Preserve all of these qualities:

- personal viewpoint;
- visible research and verifiable facts;
- a clear but restrained judgment;
- practical value without a sales tone;
- uncertainty where the evidence does not support certainty.

Do not turn the post into a product brochure, financial promotion, guaranteed recipe, securities recommendation, or step-by-step bypass guide.

## Start with one core judgment

Before drafting titles or pages, write one sentence that captures the user's actual conclusion. Include the relevant person or situation, the reason it may be worth considering, and a boundary.

Example pattern:

> 对某类有真实需求的人，这项服务值得考虑，但没有必要为了附加奖励改变原本计划。

Let this sentence control the whole post. Remove pages that do not help explain it.

## Write a judgment-led title

Combine a concrete subject with the user's judgment. Prefer calibrated words such as `值得考虑`, `可以了解`, `对我来说`, `这一阶段`, `一种选择`, or `之一`.

Useful patterns:

- `产品：我为什么会关注它`
- `人群／情境 + 产品 + 克制判断`
- `个人经历 + 一个真实问题`
- `A 与 B：我最后怎么理解`

Avoid titles that read like official documentation or make maximal claims such as `最佳`, `首选`, `必选`, `无脑冲`, `神器`, or `最划算`.

## Build the page map from real questions

Do not start by filling a fixed eight-page template. First list the 6–8 questions a person genuinely considering the service would ask. Each page must answer one question.

A common sequence is:

1. Why did I start researching it?
2. What is the main rule or visible cost?
3. What genuinely attracted me?
4. How does it differ from the main alternative?
5. Who qualifies, and what are the boundaries?
6. What is easy to misunderstand or not worth chasing?
7. What is the current situation, and how would I use it?
8. What is my final judgment, and where are the official sources?

Change the questions when the topic demands it. Do not combine fees, eligibility, rewards, market choice, and account setup on one card.

## Keep the voice human

Use first-person verbs frequently but naturally:

- `我比较了`
- `我后来发现`
- `我认真看了一下`
- `我更在意`
- `我没有急着`
- `我最后的判断是`
- `对我来说`

Put the event or comparison before the explanation. Show how the user reached a conclusion instead of announcing a universal answer.

Prefer `港股对我来说操作更直接` to `新手应该优先买港股`.

Use calibrated language: `可能`, `目前`, `倾向`, `更适合`, `比较方便`, `作为一种选择`. Avoid commands and absolutes.

## Pair professional facts with plain-language meaning

Retain necessary financial terms instead of making the post vague. Immediately explain why each unfamiliar term matters to an ordinary reader.

Examples:

- `W-8BEN` → `美股投资时需要关注的税务资料`
- `每月累计交易额 HK$250,000` → explain what that range means for the user's scale
- a product fee or eligibility threshold → explain how it changes the user's practical choice

Use a two-layer structure on cards: the exact fact first, then one short human interpretation. Do not stack unexplained terminology.

## Preserve facts and soften instructions

Financial content may still name markets, ETFs, fees, tax forms, exchange rates, taxes, eligibility, and account functions. Keep factual detail when it explains the judgment.

Convert instructions into experience or evaluation:

- Write `我研究美股时主要看了换汇、W-8BEN、交易时间和股息税。`
- Do not write `想买美股先按这5步操作。`

- Write `我当时做过这些调整，但无法判断是否与结果有关。`
- Do not write `这样改就能通过审核。`

Treat promotions as an appendix to genuine need. Use this principle:

> 奖励是附加项，不是买入或交易的理由。

## Write each card for scanning

Give each page:

- one question-like or judgment-led title;
- 2–5 information points;
- one short personal takeaway.

Put supporting detail in the body rather than shrinking the card text. When completeness conflicts with ease of reading, keep the card light and the body more complete.

Use comparison, rules, timelines, checklists, scenes, or source indexes according to the page's question. Do not repeat one four-box template across all eight pages.

## Use the Trade25 visual system

Keep a consistent editorial scrapbook language:

- warm white or ivory paper with subtle fiber or pencil texture;
- dark blue hand-drawn outlines and body text;
- coral-orange only for fees, dates, currency marks, warnings, or small checks;
- occasional pale blue, green, yellow, or lavender fills;
- blue-and-white gingham tape as a recurring series anchor;
- slightly irregular pen or colored-pencil borders;
- obvious stitched-label or notebook-style page numbers;
- two-dimensional colored-pencil information illustrations.

Use clear regular Chinese type for instructional copy and bold sans-serif for English, numbers, codes, and URLs. Never use calligraphic fonts for exact data.

Keep no more than three visual levels: large title, 2–4 core information areas, and one judgment. Avoid bank-advertising polish, business-PPT styling, 3D renders, realistic cash, stock-chart celebration, or decorative clutter.

## Make illustrations explain the point

Select illustrations that help readers classify the information:

- currency conversion → `USD ↔ HKD`;
- tax form → document plus check mark;
- trading time → clock;
- eligibility → calendar or age line;
- promotion → gift box;
- research → file, webpage, magnifier, or checklist.

Use a calm person reading, thinking, drinking coffee, checking a calendar, or observing through binoculars when the page expresses `my judgment`, `wait`, or `observe`. Do not show excitement about getting rich, counting cash, or frantic trading.

Use visual storytelling to express attitude. A page titled `奖励活动，不必急着追` should visibly communicate waiting or observing, not merely list conditions.

## Finish with judgment and sources

Prefer a final page that combines:

- who or what situation may fit;
- what may not fit;
- what the user personally plans to do;
- official product, fee, or campaign page titles;
- direct URLs and verification date;
- one short boundary sentence if necessary.

Keep closing copy natural:

> 以上是我个人的资料整理和使用理解。规则、资格和活动可能变化，操作前重新查看官方页面。

Add `不构成投资建议` only when the post genuinely discusses investment decisions. Do not repeat broad denials throughout the post.

## Final Trade25-style check

Confirm:

- The post explains why the user made this judgment, not what everyone should buy or do.
- The title contains a real scenario or calibrated personal judgment.
- Each page answers one natural reader question.
- Exact figures have an official source and a date.
- Professional terms receive a plain-language explanation.
- Alternatives are compared on relevant dimensions without declaring a universal winner.
- Promotions never become a reason to force a transaction.
- Visual layouts vary while the palette, texture, line work, and typography stay consistent.
- Illustrations explain information or attitude rather than filling space.
- `01/08 = 图1` through `08/08 = 图8`; an extra cover never shifts the mapping.
- The final result sounds like a careful friend sharing research, not a financial advertiser.
